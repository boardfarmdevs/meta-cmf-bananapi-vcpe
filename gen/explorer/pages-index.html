RDK-B · EASYMESH EVALUATION LAB

Explore the system. Try the room.

Interactive documentation for the container-based EasyMesh lab, its real Wi-Fi software, and simulated radios.

Part of the EasyMesh optimizer goal, with the prplMesh lab, the same lab on native prplMesh. The EMOSA and OpenSync labs bring existing OpenSync pods into EasyMesh. The end goal is one EasyMesh system on wmediumd, with native EasyMesh agents and OpenSync pods working together.

What it is

RDK-B EasyMesh on Banana Pi images, run as containers in one Ubuntu VM: a controller and extenders with their native EasyMesh software, a pool of 100 simulated clients, userspace wmediumd as the virtual RF medium, the EasyMesh optimizer, and an interactive room to play scenarios in.

Learn the room controls, metrics, scenarios, and live-lab operating procedures.

These public pages do not connect to or control a live lab. The explorer is a pinned documentation snapshot; topology placements are illustrative, not live measurements.

Current state

  • Development checkpoint: fresh-VM and full-suite acceptance are pending.
  • The bounded catalog diagnostics passed 24 of 24 ordinary rooms.
  • Still open: geometry branch formation and isolation/recovery (initial convergence), guarded load steering (repeat qualification), and pressure veto / weak-signal rescue (no complete live proof).
